# Provisum 2 — Film Script (transcribed from "Provisum 2 - Film Script.pdf", Oct 15 2026, @Cassian Drefke)

A 2:40 film that starts as pencil on paper and ends as Provisum 2 on glass: the story of sitting down after V1, asking what was missing, and drawing the answer.

## Concept
**Working title:** *Provided For*. Provisum is Latin for "provided for", and the film ends on that line.

**Logline:** Version 1 already worked. We sat down anyway, asked what a family would still be missing, and drew every answer by hand. Then the paper turns into glass, and the sketches become Provisum 2.

| | |
|---|---|
| Runtime | 2:40 master (16:9, 4K). Cut-downs: 0:45 (9:16) and 0:15 teaser |
| Look, Acts 1-2 | Graphite on warm off-white paper, top-down. One accent color only (the Provisum accent) for circles, ticks and arrows |
| Look, Acts 3-4 | Black void, a single moving light along the device edge, slow rotation, big type. The language of an Apple keynote product film |
| Sound | Pencil, eraser, paper slides, a pen cap. Felt piano that builds; strings; a soft pulse at the transition; one beat of silence before the reveal |
| Voice | Sparse first-person-plural VO ("we"), about 120 words in total. Hand-lettered notes on the paper do most of the talking |
| Type | Veyago house style: DM Sans for supers, Cambria for the closing line. Not Apple's typefaces |

**The one rule for the whole film: nothing appears on glass that was not first drawn on paper.** That is the story.

## Cold open and Act 1: What we had (0:00 - 0:35)
- **0:00 - Black.** Silence, then the scratch of a pencil. A single graphite line is drawn across the frame. Light comes up: it is the edge of an iPhone outline on paper.
- **0:06 - The V1 sketch.** Top-down. A hand finishes drawing Provisum 1 as it shipped: the binder for Mom, the emergency card, the deadlines, the mail, the packets. As VO names each one, the pencil ticks it.
  - **VO 1:** Provisum 1 already did a lot. Mom's papers, in one place. Every deadline, with the right reminder. The emergency card, for the night the hospital calls.
- **0:20 - The pencil stops.** The hand rests. A coffee cup is set down at the edge of the page. Hold for one breath.
  - **VO 2:** It worked. So we sat down and asked the harder question.
- **0:28 - The question.** A sticky note is pressed onto the middle of the sketch. Hand-lettered, in the accent color: **What are we still missing?**
  The music's first real chord lands as the note is smoothed flat. Cut.

## Act 2: The table (0:35 - 1:25)
Wide top-down shot of the whole desk: blank sheets, a pencil cup, an eraser, a ruler, sticky notes, an iPhone face down. Two pairs of hands. From here the film is a sketching session, one page per idea. Every page has three parts: the **drawing**, a **margin note** in the accent color, and the **sound** that marks it. Pages start slow (about 6 seconds each) and speed up (2 seconds by the end).

**VO 3:** We started with a person.

1. **"Mom isn't the only one."** The single binder tab from the V1 sketch is redrawn as a row of tabs: Mom, Dad, Me, a child, the dog. *Feature: a binder for every person in the family.*
2. **"What if the card were on her wrist?"** A watch outline; the emergency card is drawn small on its face, then a second card slides into a Wallet stack. *Feature: emergency card on Apple Watch and in Apple Wallet.*
3. **A pill bottle with a label**, a small bell above it. *Feature: medication list on the card, with refill reminders.*
4. **Three phones around one card**, a padlock drawn in the middle. Margin: "Encrypted before it leaves the phone." *Feature: share the emergency card with family.*

**VO 4:** Then we went through Mom's mail.

5. **An envelope**, a red question mark circled: "Is this a scam?" *Feature: scam check on scanned letters.*
6. **A passport**, with three tick marks on a ruler: 9, 6, 3 months. *Feature: passports and IDs for the whole family, reminded early.*
7. **Three stick figures and coins** split by dotted lines. Margin: "Who paid what?" *Feature: shared costs, exportable for the accountant.*
8. **A key and a safe deposit box.** Margin: "Where is the original?" *Feature: Where everything is.*
9. **A sound wave over a doctor's desk.** Margin: "Record it. Keep it on the phone." *Feature: visit recording with a private transcript and summary.*

**VO 5:** And we looked past one country.

10. **A rough map of Europe.** Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany and France circled; "112" and "A4" in the margin. *Feature: country-aware binders.*
11. **Five speech bubbles:** Hello, Hola, Hallo, Bonjour, Hoi. *Feature: Spanish, German, French and Dutch.*

- **1:12 - The biggest idea.** The music drops away. The hand draws a V1-style form screen, full of empty fields. Pauses. Crosses it out with one stroke. Beside it, draws the same screen again as a conversation: one question, two answer chips.
  - **VO 6:** And then the biggest one. Stop asking people to fill in forms. Just ask them.
  - *Feature: the conversation-style redesign and the new onboarding.*
- **1:20 - The wall.** Fast: the hands lay all eleven pages and the redesign into a grid on the desk. Camera rises slowly over the whole grid. Every page is now one tile of a single product.

## Act 3: The transition (1:25 - 1:55)
- **1:25 - Push in.** The camera drops into the centre tile of the grid: the redrawn binder home. The strings come in under the piano.
- **1:31 - The lines catch light.** One graphite line at a time turns into a hairline of light. The paper texture fades out underneath until only the glowing linework floats in black. This is the moment the film changes language: from the desk to the keynote.
- **1:38 - It becomes real.** The linework lifts off the page and gains depth. It is now a real iPhone, slowly rotating in a dark void. A single band of light travels along its edge. On screen, each sketched element snaps into its finished interface in the exact place it was drawn: tabs, card, deadlines, the conversation chips. Each snap lands on a beat.
- **1:48 - The family of devices.** The emergency card flips off the phone and lands on an Apple Watch face beside it, then a Wallet pass slides out behind both. The light settles.
- **1:53 - Silence.** Everything cuts to black. One full second of nothing.

## Act 4: Introducing (1:55 - 2:25)
- **1:55 - Title.** Black. Large type fades up, one word at a time: **Provisum**. Hold. Then, drawn in the accent color as if by the same pencil, a single stroke writes beside it: **2**. The music resolves.
- **2:00 - Subline.** Under it, in DM Sans: *A binder for your whole family.*
- **2:04 - The real thing, fast.** Quick cuts of genuine screen footage, each one the finished version of a page from Act 2, in the same order: the family tabs, the watch card, the refill reminder, the scam note on a letter, the passport reminder, shared costs, a visit summary, a Belgian binder dialing 112, the onboarding question with its chips.
  - **VO 7:** Everything we drew. On your iPhone. And none of it on our servers. No account. No tracking.
- **2:20 - The pair.** Hero shot: phone and watch side by side on black, light sweeping both once.

## End (2:25 - 2:40)
- **2:25 - Back to the desk.** Cut back to the paper, now in warm light. The real iPhone lies face up on the grid of sketches, showing the same binder home that is drawn underneath it. A hand sets the pencil down beside it.
  - **VO 8:** Provisum. It's Latin for *provided for*.
- **2:33 - End card.** Off-white, like the paper. "Provisum 2" and "Free on the App Store" in DM Sans. The Veyago mark small at the bottom. The last sound is the click of the pencil being put down.

## Making it (notes from the script)
- **Paper.** A3, shot top-down, daylight lamp from one side for soft shadows. Draw each page twice: once as a rough on camera, once clean for the transition trace. Keep the eraser crumbs; they read as honest.
- **The transition.** Trace the clean binder-home sketch to vectors and animate stroke-by-stroke glow. Match the vector exactly to the final screen layout so the snap from line to interface lands in place.
- **Clean app footage.** Record in the Simulator with the debug launch flags already in the project: `OB_SEED_SAMPLE=1` for a populated sample binder, `OB_SKIP_ONBOARDING=1`, `OB_APPEARANCE=dark` (sits well on black), `OB_DEMO_MEMBERS=1` for the family shot, and `OB_SAMPLE_BINDER=1` with the Simulator's region set to Belgium for the 112 shot (the sample binder follows the phone's country). Use a fictional family; no real names or numbers on screen.
- **Watch and Wallet.** Capture on real devices if possible; the Watch is the shot people will remember.
- **Music.** Licensed track with a clear build and a hard stop. Cut picture to the music, not the other way round.
- **VO.** Eight short lines. Record in a closet or a car for dead sound. Read slower than feels natural.
- **Cut-downs.** 0:45 vertical: shots 4, 6 (two pages), 9, 12, 13, 16, 18 (four cuts), 21. 0:15 teaser: shot 4, a two-second sketch montage, shot 12, black, "Provisum 2".
- Keep the Apple influence in the grammar (black void, edge light, one-word title, the beat of silence), not in the assets: **no Apple logos, event artwork or Apple typefaces.** Naming Apple Watch and Apple Wallet as features is fine.

## Open questions (from the script — decisions made in this production are logged in docs/DECISIONS.md)
- Version number: the film says "Provisum 2", but the release branch, What's New text and paywall copy say 1.2. The App Store version must ship as 2.0 before the film goes out.
- US or Europe first? Provisum is US-only today. For a US audience, shorten the Europe page and the 112 shot to one second each, or cut them for a US version.
- Your voice or text only? Both work with this script.
- Whose hands? Two pairs of hands. The author's and their assistant's would be honest.
- The quieter features (after-hospital list, checklist for after a death) are in the release but not in the film.
