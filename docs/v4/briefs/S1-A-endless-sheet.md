## Deliver (in `src/endless/**`, plus registrations in `src/previews4.ts`)
1. **`<EndlessSheet>`**: one canvas at sheet coordinates (`SHEET`, 40000 x 8000 logical px) with paper that stays seamless at every zoom, and a **camera** that applies a `CameraKey` timeline (centre, zoom, easing, from `cameraKeys(format, chapterId)`) to the whole sheet. Chapters mount as children placed at `REGIONS`; only regions whose bounds touch the viewport grown by 20% mount (BIBLE4 1.6). Provide `useSheetCamera(chapterId)` and a pure `cameraAt(format, masterSeconds)` so chapters and tools can ask where the camera is.
2. **`<PencilLine>`**: the unbroken line as ONE path, revealed by master time, with the pencil tip leading it, using the v1 engine's pencil physics (graphite grain, pressure, wobble) so it looks like a pencil at any zoom. Line width in screen terms follows `LINE_WIDTH` (it must never vanish at the far zoom). It must be able to change ink per voice (graphite, accent, sepia; sepia is added by S1-B in `src/paper/types.ts`, so take the ink as a prop and stub sepia until S1-B lands).
3. **Level of detail** exactly per BIBLE4 1.6: near / mid / far layers, a `far` image path and the chapter-PNG convention (`public/tex/sheet/<chapterId>.png`), a script `tools/renderSheetLod.ts` that renders those images from the chapters (placeholders are fine now), and the rule that a static region never re-runs the pencil canvas.
4. **Compositions** (register in `src/previews4.ts`): `Sheet4-wide` and `Sheet4-tall`, 600 s, showing the sheet with placeholder chapter marks (a number and a title drawn by the engine at each region), the unbroken line travelling through all 11 regions, and the camera following `CAMERA` for the whole film, including the 0:18 pull-back and the 9:30 pull-back.
5. **Proof, not claims.** Render stills at every chapter hand-off and at 0:18 and 9:30 in both formats (scale 1, plus a few at scale 2 for line quality) into `out/review/s1a/`; run `validateSheet4()`; write down the measured render time per frame (near, mid, far) in `src/endless/README.md`, with how to use the components. Look at your stills and fix what looks wrong before reporting. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `zsh tools/checkRules.sh` must pass.
