# A2: the character art pass (cloud, Opus or Fable, effort high, CAP $12)

Gate 1 verdict on the S1-B characters: the system works (continuous line, rough pass then clean draw-on, sepia Maya, one accent detail each, the dog is recognisable and charming) but the **figures are stiff**: boxy torsos with hard shoulders, near-symmetrical poses, seated figures that read as sacks with no chair and a sack-like hip-to-seat join, `tap` has a long forearm, Dad's `lie` is a placeholder, Leo's cap brim is a floating accent stroke, and the character design sheet's clothing details are mostly missing (Maya's cardigan, sleeves pushed up and hair knot; Dad's stoop, collar and slippers). This is the film's biggest art risk: Maya carries chapters 2, 8, 9 and 10.

Read first: `CONTRACT.md` (section 13), `docs/v4/BIBLE4.md` sections 3 and 4 (design sheet, poses, flipbook swaps) and the chapter beat sheets where characters appear (5.2, 5.8, 5.9, 5.10), `src/characters/README.md` and `src/characters/**` (the skeleton, the outline builder, the poses), `src/paper/README.md`.

Branch `claude/a2-character-art-pass`, open a PR to `main`. Cloud rules (CONTRACT 13.7): stills only, never commit `package-lock.json`, no sub-agents, do not edit `src/previews4.ts`. **Spend cap $12: three render-and-look rounds at most; stop when the deliverables are done; report.**

## Deliver (in `src/characters/**` only; keep the public API stable: character ids, pose ids, props)
1. **Life in the figures.** Draw them like quick gesture sketches by a skilled illustrator who loves their subjects: a slight S-curve through the spine, weight on one leg, a tilted head, sloped and uneven shoulders, limbs of varying width with soft joints (not rectangles), hands as simple mitten shapes with a hint of thumb, feet that sit on the ground. Faces stay minimal (3-5 strokes, no pupils) but must read warm and specific per character: the brow and mouth carry the personality. Adults must not look like each other.
2. **Clothing and silhouette from the design sheet**, as part of the single continuous outline where possible: Maya's loose hair knot, cardigan hem and sleeves pushed up; Dad's slight stoop, collar and slippers; Leo's cap (the brim must be part of the cap outline, not a floating stroke) and backpack; Mia's ponytail, dress and mismatched socks; Jonas's three-stroke beard, rolled sleeves and watch strap; Sam's scarf, shoulder bag and phone; Biscuit stays as is unless a flaw shows.
3. **Seated poses** (`sit-A`, `sit-B`, the table poses): they must sit ON something. Draw a simple chair or stool line as part of the pose (or an explicit prop segment), a believable hip-to-seat join, thighs foreshortened, forearm on the table foreshortened. Fix `tap` (forearm length) and replace Dad's `lie` with a real reclining pose.
4. **Proof.** Re-render `CharacterSheet-wide` and `Maya-poses-wide` (scale 2, full-resolution crops) into `out/review/a2/` after each round and LOOK; keep a before/after strip of Maya in three poses for the owner. Update the draw-on so the new outline still draws as one pencil movement with the rough pass under it. Do not hide a character that still fails: say which and why.
5. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `zsh tools/checkRules.sh` must pass; update `src/characters/README.md`.
