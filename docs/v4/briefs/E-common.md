# Wave E: the second art-director pass, common rules (read this first, then your own brief)

Read `docs/v4/briefs/C-common.md` first: every rule there applies (cloud stills only, your own folders only, before/after stills, two render-and-look rounds, PR to `main`, do not merge, report). Wave E is driven by the whole-film sweep in `docs/v4/QA1.md` (ranked defects, contract sweep, owner questions) and by `docs/v4/MASTER4-AUDIT.md`.

## What is different in wave E
1. **Your rows.** Your brief lists the QA1 defect numbers you own. Fix them, then spend what is left of your cap on the next worst things you find yourself (render 20 frames per format of each chapter you own and judge them like an art director: what would a viewer on a phone fail to read, miss, or find ugly?). End your report with a table: defect number, fixed / partly / not, and the master second you re-checked.
2. **Readability rule.** Anything meant to be read must be at least 28 px tall on a 1080 px wide frame (tall) and at least 36 px tall on a 1920 px wide frame (wide) at the moment it is read. Text that is texture (the Xcode navigator rows, footage text inside real app screens) may stay small. Nothing meant to be read may touch or cross the pencil line, sit on top of art it fights with, or cross the frame edge or the central 1080 px column in tall.
3. **Prove it on the master.** The film is `Master4-wide` / `-tall` (one sheet, one camera). After a fix, re-render the same master seconds QA1 used (master second = chapter start + local second; `slotOf4(id).start`) in BOTH formats and LOOK. Chapter-alone compositions are a convenience only.
4. **Owner decisions are not yours.** Words that state a fact about the app or the company (`0 servers`, `Free on the App Store`, the invitation wording, the test count, `Private by design`, the Veyago mark, the date on chapter 3) stay exactly as they are; layout, size and placement of those words are yours. The words are being settled in `docs/v4/QA1.md` section 3 by the owner.
5. **Shared files.** `src/beats4.ts`, `docs/SCRIPT-10MIN-v4.md`, `docs/v4/voicelines.json`, `src/edits4.ts` belong to session E6 only; `src/sheet4.ts` camera keys belong to session E5 only for chapter 11. If you need a change in a file you do not own, put the exact edit in the report.
