# A1: the shared chapter kit (cloud, Opus, effort high, CAP $12)

Every chapter needs the same few pieces. Build them once so the chapter sessions that follow do not each invent their own.

Read first: `CONTRACT.md` (section 13), `docs/v4/BIBLE4.md` sections 1.6, 3 (inks, supers) and the beat sheets (section 5) for how supers, number rolls and screens on paper are used, `src/endless/README.md` (camera, `useSheetCamera`, `PencilLine`), `src/characters/README.md`, `src/popup/README.md`, `src/paper/README.md` (lettering, pencil physics), `src/beats4.ts` (`SUPERS`, `INK4`, `RECORDINGS`), `src/film4.ts`, `public/footage/manifest.json` and `public/footage4/manifest4.json`.

Branch `claude/a1-kit`, open a PR to `main`. Cloud rules (CONTRACT 13.7): stills only, never commit `package-lock.json`, no sub-agents, do not edit `src/previews4.ts` (put previews in your own folder's `previews.ts` and export them; see `src/chapters/registry.ts` for how chapter previews are collected, and add your kit previews through `src/kit/previews.ts` plus ONE additive line in `src/previews4.ts`). **Spend cap $12: at most two render-and-look rounds per component; stop when the deliverables are done; report.**

## Deliver (in `src/kit/**`)
1. **`<Super>`**: text written by the pencil line itself in the speaking voice's ink (handwriting, using the v1 engine's lettering), then resolving to DM Sans (the film's super face, BIBLE4 3). Props: text, ink (`graphite|accent|sepia`), start time, write duration, hold, anchor (a point in sheet coordinates or a lower-third position), size. It must read in both formats and stay legible at the working zoom of its region (it scales with the camera or sits in screen space: decide per BIBLE4 and document). Deterministic.
2. **`<NumberRoll>`**: an odometer in the accent colour: digits roll to a target string such as `4.3(a)`, `21 September 2026`, `28 September 2026`, `6 kinds of people`; non-digit characters stay still. Props: from, to, start, duration, ink size.
3. **`<ScreenOnPaper>`**: a drawn laptop or phone frame (pencil, via the engine, with the slight wobble around it) with a video clip or poster masked inside. Props: device (`laptop|phone`), source (`OffthreadVideo` with a hot window, or a still), `near` (video decodes only when the region is near; otherwise the poster), scale. Use the real clips' native aspect (phone clips are 1206x2622). No Apple logo on the drawn laptop.
4. **A chapter template**: `src/kit/ChapterShell.tsx` (or hook) that gives a chapter its local time, its camera (`useSheetCamera`), the `cues` registration pattern, and a documented folder layout (`Chapter.tsx`, `cues.ts`, `previews.ts` exported from the folder's `index.ts`; see `ChapterModule` in `src/chapters/registry.ts`). Replace ONE stub chapter as the worked example only if it costs under $2: otherwise just document the template.
5. **Proof and docs**: `Kit-demo-wide` and `Kit-demo-tall` showing a Super writing and resolving in each ink, a NumberRoll mid-roll and finished, and a ScreenOnPaper laptop and phone with real footage; stills at scale 1 (and one at scale 2) in `out/review/a1/`; `src/kit/README.md` with usage, measured render time per frame, and how chapter owners should use each piece. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `zsh tools/checkRules.sh` must pass.
