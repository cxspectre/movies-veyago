# Wave B: chapter sessions, common rules (read this first, then your own brief)

You build ONE chapter (or two) of the 10-minute film, replacing the stub in `src/chapters/<id>/`. The foundations exist and are merged: the endless sheet and camera (`src/endless`), the pop-up paper and effects (`src/popup`, `src/popup/effects`), the line characters (`src/characters`), the shared kit (`src/kit`: `Super`, `NumberRoll`, `ScreenOnPaper`, `ChapterShell`) and the v1 pencil engine (`src/paper`). Use them; do not reinvent them. If one has a gap, work around it in your chapter folder and say so in your report.

## Read first
`CONTRACT.md` (section 13), `docs/v4/PLAN.md`, `docs/v4/BIBLE4.md` (sections 1-3, your chapter's beat sheet in section 5, and 7 for the sound cues), `docs/SCRIPT-10MIN-v4.md` (your chapter's paragraph: the exact words), `src/kit/README.md`, `src/endless/README.md`, `src/popup/README.md`, `src/characters/README.md`, `src/paper/README.md`, `src/sheet4.ts` (your region, `LINE_ANCHORS`, `handoff()`, the camera keys for both formats), `src/beats4.ts` (your chapter's constants, `SUPERS`, `VO4`, cue needs), `docs/v4/CLAIMS.md` (every word, number and date you put on screen must be a row there; if it is unconfirmed, use a clearly drawn placeholder and flag it in your report), `public/footage/manifest.json` and `public/footage4/manifest4.json`.

## Rules
Cloud rules of CONTRACT 13.7 apply: stills only (no video renders), `FILM_GL=swangle` if you touch WebGL, never commit `package-lock.json`, no sub-agents, do not edit `src/previews4.ts` or `src/chapters/registry.ts` (your chapter folder exports `previews` and `cues` itself). Work on your branch and open a PR to `main`. Nothing on glass that was not first drawn on paper. The first app is never named; the plain-English form of its name is banned. The design file is imagined (draw it). App Store Connect, store results, reviews and terminal text are DRAWN with the exact words (13.5). The family is fictional. Maya's ink is sepia, the Founder's graphite, the Maker's accent (BIBLE4 section 3).

## Deliver
1. `src/chapters/<id>/`: `Chapter.tsx` (an `Art` in sheet coordinates, an `Overlay` in screen space, and `Chapter = <ChapterShell .../>`), `cues.ts` (the COMPLETE foley cue list, module-local seconds, from the sound needs in BIBLE4 5.x and 7.4; the v4 sound names fold, scissors, crumple, stamp, string, pin, odometer, train, plane, rocket, marble_run, padlock exist), `previews.ts` if you need extra stills, `index.ts` exporting all of them. Every beat time comes from `src/beats4.ts`; never hard-code a time it holds.
2. Both formats from the same Art: the essential art sits in the central 1080 px column (BIBLE4 1.7); the camera is global (`sheet4.ts`), you own only your region's art and timing. Your art must begin and end exactly where the line hands off (`handoff(id)`).
3. Proof: render stills of `Chapter-<id>-wide` and `-tall` at the beat times that matter (the start, each major beat, the end) at scale 1 into `out/review/<brief-id>/` (a contact sheet via `tools/stills.ts --sheet`), LOOK at them, and fix what looks wrong. At most two render-and-look rounds. Check two adjacent frames of any fast motion for flicker.
4. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `zsh tools/checkRules.sh` must pass.
5. Report: files, how verified, the measured render time per frame, what is weak or unfinished, and every claim or placeholder the owner must confirm.
