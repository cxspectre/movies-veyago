## Build
1. **`tools/captions4.ts`**: writes `.srt` and `.vtt` caption files (sidecar files for YouTube; not burned in) for the 16:9 master and for `Hook75`, `Cut180` and each short, from the voice-over lines in `VO4` (the spoken words; supers are not captions). Timing: each line starts at its beat time; it ends at the real duration of its recorded stem when `audio/v4/vo/<id>.wav` exists (read the WAV header), else at the estimate in `beats4.ts`; for the cut-downs re-time through the EDL segments (reuse `tools/mix/edlSegments` / `src/audio/sheet4.ts` slicing). Break lines at 42 characters, two lines at most, 1 to 7 seconds per cue, never across a cut. Output to `out/captions/` (git-ignored) and document the command in `docs/v4/PUBLISH.md`; commit the master's `.vtt` and `.srt` under `docs/v4/captions/` as a sample. Add tests.
2. **`docs/v4/PUBLISH.md`**: for YouTube (16:9 master): three title options, a description (the first two lines are the hook; the chapter timestamps from `SLOTS4`; credits lines marked PENDING where the owner must decide: synthetic voices, fictional family), tags, a pinned comment; for TikTok and YouTube Shorts: a caption and hashtags for the hook, the 3:00 cut and each of the 11 shorts, and the order and days to post them. EVERY factual statement must correspond to a row of `docs/v4/CLAIMS.md`; mark any that is UNCONFIRMED and leave it out of the text rather than guess. Never name the first app or its plain-English form; no launch date and no build time.
3. **Thumbnails**: compositions `Thumb-wide-a|b|c` (1280x720) and `Cover-tall-a|b|c` (1080x1920) made from the film's own drawings and type (the pencil line, Maya's house or the village, the title "Provisum 2" in the film's end-card type; no Apple logo, no app screens you did not draw, no first-app name), legible at 160 px wide (the YouTube list size). Render stills at scale 1 into `out/review/h2/` and LOOK; check they read as small thumbnails (downscale a copy to 160 px and look).
